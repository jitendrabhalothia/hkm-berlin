What is Ratha Yatra?

Ratha Yatra — the Festival of the Chariots — is one of the most ancient and joyful celebrations in the world, originating in Puri, Odisha, India, over 5,000 years ago. Lord Jagannath (meaning "Lord of the Universe"), along with His brother Lord Baladeva and sister Subhadra Devi, are placed on ornate wooden chariots and pulled through the streets by thousands of devotees.

Śrīla Prabhupāda, the founder of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), brought Ratha Yatra to the Western world in 1967 in San Francisco — a historic moment when tens of thousands joined for the first time outside India. Today, Ratha Yatra is celebrated in hundreds of cities worldwide, including London, New York, Sydney — and now, magnificently, in Berlin.

Berlin Ratha Yatra 2026 — The Biggest in Berlin's History

The Berlin Ratha Yatra 2026 on 18 July was a landmark moment for the Hare Krishna community in Germany. With over 1,200 participants, it stands as the largest Ratha Yatra festival ever organised in Berlin.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ratha Yatra?

Ratha Yatra (Festival of the Chariots) is an ancient Hindu festival celebrating Lord Jagannath. Devotees pull a wooden chariot through the streets while chanting the Hare Krishna Maha Mantra. It originated in Puri, Odisha over 5,000 years ago and is now celebrated in hundreds of cities worldwide.

When was Berlin Ratha Yatra 2026?

18 July 2026 at Sri Ganesha Hindu Temple, Hasenheide 106, Berlin.

How many people attended?

Over 1,200 devotees and well-wishers attended, making it the biggest Ratha Yatra festival in Berlin's history.

Who organised it?

Hare Krishna Berlin Devotees — a community following the teachings of Śrīla Prabhupāda. Website: bhaktiyogagermany.de
