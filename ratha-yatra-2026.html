All Glories to Śrīla Prabhupāda

Thank You — Hare Krishna! 🙏

❀

By the unlimited mercy of Lord Jagannath and His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Śrīla Prabhupāda, the Berlin Ratha Yatra 2026 was a magnificent celebration of love, devotion and community.

Over 1,200 devotees and well-wishers joined us on 18 July 2026 at Sri Ganesha Hindu Temple, Hasenheide 106, Berlin — chanting the Hare Krishna Maha Mantra, pulling Lord Jagannath's chariot through the streets, and sharing the joy of prasadam together.

A heartfelt thanks to our 132 dedicated volunteers across 10 seva teams — Security, Decoration, Kirtan, Food Cooking, Food Serving, Puja Seva, Logistics, Welcome, Media and Cleanup — who gave their time and energy with full devotion.

"The holy name of the Lord is so powerful that simply by chanting it one can be freed from all the reactions of sinful life, and certainly anyone who does so is never again devoured by the blazing fire of material existence."

— Śrīla Prabhupāda, Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 6.2.46

All glories to Lord Jagannath! All glories to Śrīla Prabhupāda! Hare Krishna! 🙏
