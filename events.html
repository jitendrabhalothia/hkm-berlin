About Janmashtami

Janmashtami is the divine appearance day of Lord Sri Krishna — the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Celebrated on the 8th day of Krishna Paksha in the month of Bhadrapada, devotees fast, chant and celebrate all night until midnight — the moment of Krishna's appearance. Śrīla Prabhupāda described this day as one of the most auspicious of the entire year.

Janmashtami Quiz 🪔

How well do you know Lord Krishna — based on Śrīla Prabhupāda's teachings?

Question 1 of 4

What does "Janmashtami" mean in Sanskrit?

🪔 "Janma" means appearance/birth and "Ashtami" means the 8th day of the lunar fortnight. Śrīla Prabhupāda taught: "Kṛṣṇa's appearance in this world is not like ordinary birth. He appears by His own internal potency, ātma-māyā." — Bhagavad-gītā 4.6 purport

In which city was Lord Sri Krishna born?

🏛️ Lord Krishna appeared in the prison cell of Kamsa in Mathura. Śrīla Prabhupāda explains: "Kṛṣṇa descended into the prison house of Mathurā in the dead of night, 5,000 years ago, and immediately left for Gokula Vṛndāvana." — Kṛṣṇa Book, Chapter 3

What is the most important activity Śrīla Prabhupāda prescribed for Janmashtami?

🎶 Śrīla Prabhupāda taught: "On Janmāṣṭamī, the devotees observe a fast throughout the day and spend the night chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa, hearing the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa's birth, and worshipping the Deity." — The fast is broken at midnight, the moment of Krishna's appearance!

Śrīla Prabhupāda said: "Simply by chanting one holy name of Kṛṣṇa, one can attain..." — what?

🌟 Śrīla Prabhupāda wrote: "The holy name of Kṛṣṇa is so powerful that simply by chanting it, one can be freed from all the reactions of sinful life." (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 6.2.46 purport). The Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra is the greatest gift of Kali-yuga!
